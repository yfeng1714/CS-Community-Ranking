# ADR 0000: Short decision title

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Owners:** Project owner and implementation reviewer

## Context

Describe the problem, relevant product intent, constraints, and why a durable decision is needed.

## Decision

State the chosen option precisely, including what is and is not part of the decision.

## Alternatives considered

List credible alternatives and why they were not selected.

## Consequences

Record positive consequences, tradeoffs, migration implications, and follow-up work.

## Validation

List the tests, measurements, or review gate that will validate the decision.
